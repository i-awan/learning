Wiz is a cloud-native security platform providing continuous, agentless visibility across cloud infrastructure and containerised workloads. It delivers integrated capabilities including container vulnerability scanning, cloud security posture management, identity risk analysis, secrets detection, and contextual attack-path prioritisation within a unified risk model. By integrating directly with cloud control planes and registries, Wiz reduces operational overhead associated with per-node agents while enabling scalable coverage across multi-cloud and hybrid environments. Its strength lies in correlating vulnerabilities with exposure and privilege context, allowing prioritisation based on actual risk rather than raw CVE volume.

The purpose of adopting Wiz is to standardise container and cloud security scanning across platforms under a scalable, continuous assessment model. Wiz will act as the authoritative source of vulnerability findings, while the internal scanner will remain the central policy enforcement and CI/CD decision engine. Integration will be achieved via controlled API or event-driven ingestion, ensuring the internal scanner consumes validated findings without tight coupling to vendor runtime operations.

Problem Statement – Existing AquaSec Deployment

The current Aqua Enterprise implementation relies on Kubernetes DaemonSet enforcers and a node-based licensing model, resulting in cost sensitivity to cluster growth and operational overhead related to agent lifecycle management. The existing pattern requires scan initiation via Aqua APIs, creating tighter coupling and potential scalability constraints in high-volume CI/CD environments. Additionally, the self-managed console database introduces retention and capacity management responsibilities. These factors limit scalability and increase operational complexity in elastic cloud environments, necessitating a more streamlined and context-driven security approach.
