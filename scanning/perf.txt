WIZ SCANNING PLATFORM
Performance Requirements & Acceptance Criteria
Version 1.0  |  Confidential  |  For Wiz Account Team


1. Purpose & Scope
This document defines the minimum performance requirements and acceptance criteria that Wiz must satisfy to support our image scanning workload. These requirements apply to both the Wiz SaaS scanning endpoint and the Wiz Outpost Lite deployment running on our internal on-premises Kubernetes cluster. The criteria set out herein form the basis of our production readiness assessment and must be validated through formal performance testing prior to go-live.
The scanner handler service acts as the intermediary layer between our CI/CD pipelines and the Wiz scanning platform. All scan requests are routed through the scanner handler, which manages queuing, rate limiting, deduplication, and result retrieval. The performance requirements defined in this document must be met end-to-end — from submission of a scan request to the scanner handler through to completion and retrieval of findings from Wiz.

8. Non-Negotiable Requirements
The following requirements are absolute prerequisites for production sign-off. They are not subject to negotiation or deferral. Failure to satisfy any one of these items will prevent production cutover regardless of performance test outcomes in other areas.

	•	Wiz must formally confirm the per-tenant GraphQL API rate limit in writing before integration testing begins.
	•	Wiz must provide Outpost Lite resource benchmark data from internal lab testing before namespace sizing is finalised.
	•	Wiz must confirm that scan job durability is maintained across SaaS incidents — no submitted jobs are silently lost during an outage.
	•	Wiz must confirm the licence model for duplicate digest submissions — one digest must count as one licence unit regardless of how many scan requests reference it.
	•	Wiz must provide a sandbox environment representative of production for phases one and two of acceptance testing.
	•	Wiz must confirm whether Private Service Connect or equivalent private connectivity is supported for on-premises to Wiz SaaS traffic, given the scanner handler runs on-premises and current egress routes through a shared corporate proxy.



These requirements form the minimum bar for production readiness. Wiz is requested to confirm acceptance of each requirement in writing, or raise any constraints or dependencies at the earliest opportunity.
